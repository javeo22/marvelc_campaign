P|Normal|Before Every Issue
TABLE
1 || Use the required hero, villain, modular set, and tier. Pick an aspect that hero has not used earlier in the main story.
2 || Apply issue pre-setup/set-aside instructions, then complete normal scenario setup.
3 || Resolve campaign setup, continuity flags, Interlude choice, Network setup effects, and Final Preparation if Issue 15.
4 || Choose Field Assets: normally 1; if Network 6+, choose 2. At 15+ Intel, Endgame Protocol does not count against the limit in Issue 15.
5 || Draw and mulligan. If Network 6+, then deal yourself the Prepared Ambush facedown encounter card.
ENDTABLE
P|Normal|After the Game
TABLE
RECORD IT NOW / Win: +1 Intel. Objective: +1 Intel. First-time Hero Mastery: +1 Intel. Hero defeat: +1 Network and 1 Scar. Main-scheme loss: +2 Network. Objectives and Mastery remain recorded even if you later lose. A Scar lowers only the starting hit-point dial by 1; it does not lower maximum HP. Remove 1 Scar when that hero wins.
ENDTABLE

P|Normal|FIELD ASSETS & MASTERIES
P|Normal|Field Assets are out-of-play campaign references. They are not upgrades/supports and cannot be targeted by encounter effects.
TABLE
INTEL || ASSET || ONCE-PER-GAME EFFECT
3 || Emergency Reserve || Reduce the cost of any card by 1.
3 || Field Medic || Heal 2 damage from your identity.
6 || S.H.I.E.L.D. Recon || Before encounter cards are dealt, look at the top 2 encounter cards; return them in either order.
6 || Damage Control || After you defeat a side scheme, remove 2 threat from the main scheme.
9 || Countermeasure Suite || After a boost card flips, blank 1 printed boost icon for that activation; not boost-star text.
9 || Reactive Reflex || After your hero defends against an attack, ready your hero.
12 || Stark-Wakanda Interface || Reduce an upgrade or support cost by 2, minimum 0.
12 || Avengers Emergency Channel || After a basic ATK or THW, ready that hero and deal 1 damage to it.
15 || Endgame Protocol || Issue 15 only. Interrupt: when a treachery is revealed, cancel its When Revealed effects; not Surge.
ENDTABLE

ASSET LIMIT / Normally equip 1 unlocked Asset. At Network 6+, equip 2. Endgame Protocol does not count against this limit in Issue 15 if you have 15+ Intel.
