P|Normal|Unlocked by Intel. Equip one per issue; at Network 6+, equip two.

P|Normal|When your Intel reaches a threshold, both assets at that threshold become unlocked permanently. At the beginning of each issue, after choosing your deck, choose one unlocked Field Asset and place its reference card beside your identity. If you begin an issue at Network 6 or higher, choose two instead. Unless stated otherwise, each Field Asset can be used once per game; turn it facedown after use. Field Assets are out-of-play campaign reference cards: they are not upgrades or supports, cannot be discarded or targeted by encounter-card effects, and do not count as cards you control.
